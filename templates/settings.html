Daily check schedule

Timezone: Pacific/Auckland. Automatically follows NZST and NZDT. Domains queue at this time and are checked one at a time.

Check now runs an additional check without moving the daily schedule. New and edited domains also receive an immediate check.

Saving applies the next future occurrence to all domains. If the server misses a scheduled run, one catch-up check runs when the worker resumes.

At the daylight-saving jump, a nonexistent time runs at the first valid minute. A repeated time runs only once.
